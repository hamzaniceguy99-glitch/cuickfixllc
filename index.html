How we work

A Florida shop, honest about the trade-offs

Two things worth knowing before any repair. First, a third-party repair can void whatever is left of a manufacturer warranty, and on some devices features tied to the original factory component may stop working after a screen or battery change — regardless of how good the replacement part is. Second, please back up your own data. We take care, but no repair shop can guarantee data, and no data recovery attempt is ever guaranteed to succeed.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Why does the diagnosis come first?

Because quoting without opening a device is guessing. It is free here, and it sometimes ends with us telling you the repair costs more than the device is worth.

Will Face ID still work after a screen replacement?

On some devices, features tied to the original factory part can be reduced or stop working after any screen replacement. We will tell you what applies to your specific model before fitting anything.

Does this void my warranty?

A third-party repair can void whatever manufacturer warranty remains. If your device is still under warranty, it is usually worth going to the manufacturer first, and we will say so.

My phone got wet. Should I put it in rice?

No, that does nothing. Power it off, do not charge it, and bring it in. Charging a wet device is what turns a recoverable one into a dead one.

Can you get my photos back?

We can attempt it. Nobody can promise it. Stop using the device now — every hour it runs reduces what is recoverable — and we will tell you honestly if it needs a specialist lab.

Should I back up before bringing it in?

Yes, always, wherever you take it. We take reasonable care, but no shop can guarantee your data survives a repair.
